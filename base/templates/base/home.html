Hi I'm Jack


I'm a Bioinformatician looking to get intimate with your data.

Bioinformatician & Machine Learning Engineer

Having completed my Masters in Computer Science specialized in Artificial Intelligence in 2022 from the University of Freiburg, Germany, I began working as a bioinformatics consultant using my knowledge of single-cell RNA sequencing tools to analyze the gene expression profiles of new drugs as they move through clinical trials. Currently I am Lead Bioinformatician for the Translational Oncology Research Center at the Vrije Universiteit Brussel (VUB).

I've worked with 5 separate organizations doing various analyses ranging from differential gene expression detection for both bulk- and single-cell RNA seq data, investigating chromatin accessibility, identifying key mutations in variant calling format data, performing differential splicing analyses to understand genes affected by alterations to the spliceosome, as well as other tasks related to biological data analysis. More recently, I've been developing machine learning models to determine the key features involved in severe fibrotic liver tissues and the features involved in the shrinking of tumors given drug treatment. These models work to learn the most important relationships between genes in order to understand how novel drugs can reduce fibrosis and cancer.


Experience & Education

I completed my Bachelor's in Mathematics in 2019 at the University of Alberta and my Masters in Computer Science from the University of Freiburg, Germany in 2022. Specialized in artificial intelligence, I've focused my education on the various machine learning models at the forefront of the field and how to refine them to be able to tackle any task. My thesis in particular was on developing a novel method for projecting high-dimensional single-cell time-series datasets in the field of bioinformatics to produce more meaningful embeddings.

I've worked at multiple bioinformatics labs throughout my studies and most recently have worked as a bioinformatics & machine learning consultant in pharmaceuticals analysing multi-omics data to discover the mechanisms of novel drugs in the treatment of non-fatty alcoholic liver disease and various cancers. Currently I am the Lead Bioinformatician for the VUB's Translational Oncology Research Center in Brussels, Belgium.

What I Do

I work daily in Python and R and utilize their packages a variety of tasks ranging from RNA sequencing analyses, differential expression testing, pathway enrichment analyses and Gene Set Enrichment Analyses (GSEA), and Weighted Gene Correlational Network Analyses (WGCNA), among others. I'm specialized in single-cell transcriptomics and have experience analyzing modern spatial transcriptomic datasets as well.

I am specialized in Machine Learning and have used it on a wide range of different and unique projects; from using random forest models to identify gene signatures for novel drugs in development, an image recognition program aimed at detecting cattle in images and quantifying their stress levels automatically, and even for a personal project with the goal of predicting NHL hockey games based on available player statistics.

It is also important to be able to communicate the methodologies and results of all of these unique projects and analyses and thus I am also very well versed in being able to simply communicate complex topics as well as create easy visuals to convey results using software like matplotlib, seaborn, plotly, ggplot, and others.
